Rules used

  • Qualifying rate: greater of 5.25% or contract rate plus 2%: OSFI
  • GDS up to 39% and TDS up to 44%; half of condo fees count: CMHC
  • Minimum down payment: 5% of the first $500,000, 10% of the portion from $500,000 to $1.5M, 20% at $1.5M and above: FCAC
  • Semi-annual compounding on fixed-rate mortgages: Interest Act
  • Document freshness (30-day pay stub, 90-day down payment history) reflects common lender practice and varies by lender.
